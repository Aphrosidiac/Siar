This policy covers two groups: customers who use Siar (developers and their teams), and end users who connect their social accounts through a Siar-powered flow, such as a hosted connect link, in a product built by a Siar customer. For the second group, the customer decides why and how the data is used and Siar processes it on the customer's behalf; we describe our side below.

## What we collect

**From customers**
- Account details: name, email address, organization name and a password hash.
- Billing details: plan, invoices and a payment-provider reference. Card numbers are handled by our payment processor, not stored by us.
- Usage and security records: API requests by key, quotas used, audit log entries, IP address and user agent for sign-in and security.
- Content you send: posts, comments, uploaded media, webhook endpoints and settings.

**From connected social accounts**
- Access and refresh tokens issued by the network. They are encrypted at rest and never returned by the API.
- Profile data the network returns: account id, username, display name, avatar, bio, selected channel and, where the network provides it, email address.
- Content and metrics you ask Siar to read: comments on your posts, post and account analytics, and, where you enable messaging, conversations.

We request only the permissions listed on each [platform page](/platforms).

## Data from Facebook, Instagram and Threads

When a business connects a Facebook Page, an Instagram professional account or a Threads profile, Siar receives data from Meta through the permissions the business grants in Meta's login:

- **The connected account:** its id, name or username, profile picture, the Pages, businesses and Instagram accounts the person chose to share, and the access token (encrypted).
- **Its content and insights:** the posts Siar publishes for it and their ids, and the reach, views, likes and comments counts Meta reports for the account and its posts.
- **People who interact with it:** for comments and replies on the account's posts, the comment text and the commenter's id, name or username and, where Meta provides it, profile picture. Where the business turns on messages, the messages people send to the Page or Instagram account, with the sender's id, name and profile picture.

Siar uses this data only to show it to the business's own team in Siar and to carry out what that team asks: publishing, replying, hiding or deleting comments, answering messages and showing insights. It is never sold, never shared with other customers, never used for advertising, and never used to train machine-learning models. Siar sends a message or reply only when a person on the business's team writes it, or when the business has set up a reply rule for its own account.

The data is deleted when the business disconnects the account, when access is removed in Facebook or Instagram settings (Meta notifies Siar and the account is deleted after the grace period above), or on request. A person who commented on or messaged a business can ask us to delete that data at [{CONTACT_EMAIL}](mailto:{CONTACT_EMAIL}). See [data deletion](/data-deletion).

## Why we use it

To provide the service (publish, schedule, read comments and analytics, deliver webhooks), to bill and support you, to keep the service secure and prevent abuse, to meet legal obligations, and to improve reliability. We do not sell personal data and we do not use data from connected social accounts for advertising or to train machine-learning models.

## How long we keep it

- **Tokens** are deleted when an account is disconnected or removed. After a network revokes access, the account is flagged and removed after a grace period set on the organization (72 hours by default), and its tokens are erased then.
- **Uploaded media** is kept for {RESOURCE_LIMITS.mediaRetentionDays} days after its last use; deleted uploads keep their file for {RESOURCE_LIMITS.softDeletedMediaDays} more days. Media on a draft or scheduled post is kept until the post finishes.
- **Posts, comments and analytics** are kept while the organization exists, so you can read your history, and can be deleted through the API or on request.
- **Account and billing records** are kept as long as the account is open and for the period tax and accounting law requires.

## Who else handles it

We use service providers to run Siar: a payment processor for subscriptions and invoices, a cloud storage and delivery provider for media, infrastructure and email delivery providers. They process data only to provide those services to us. We disclose data to the social networks you connect only as needed to carry out your requests, and to authorities where the law requires it.

## Where it is processed

Siar is operated from Malaysia and our providers may process data in other countries. Where required we use appropriate safeguards for transfers.

## Security

Tokens and secrets are encrypted at rest, traffic uses HTTPS, API keys are stored hashed, requests are isolated per organization, and access to production data is limited. No system is perfectly secure; report a concern to [{CONTACT_EMAIL}](mailto:{CONTACT_EMAIL}).

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act and, where applicable, GDPR), you may ask to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. To delete data tied to a connected social account, see [data deletion](/data-deletion). For anything else, email us; we may need to verify who you are, and we reply within 30 days. If you are an end user of a Siar customer's product, please contact that customer first; we will help them respond.

## Cookies

The website uses no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. The dashboard uses a session cookie to keep you signed in, and your browser stores a theme preference locally.

## Children

Siar is not directed to children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data.
