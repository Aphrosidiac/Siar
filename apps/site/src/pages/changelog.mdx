## November 2026

- **Starter plan:** $19 a month (RM39 in Malaysia) for one brand and 300 posts a month, with analytics.
- **Network status, everywhere from one source:** every page, `llms.txt` and `pricing.md` read each network's status from one record. See [networks](/platforms) and the dated [approval record](/platforms/approvals).
- **Facebook and Instagram DMs** and comment-to-DM automations: in beta, turned on per organization on request (paid plans). Meta approved the messaging permissions on 27 October 2026.

- **Sign in with Google:** create an account, sign in or accept an invitation with Google, next to email and password. Connect or disconnect Google, and set a password, under Your account in the dashboard.

## v1.0 · October 2026

The first release of the Siar API.

- **Built for {NETWORKS.length} networks:** {NETWORKS.map((n) => n.name).join(', ')}. Each opens as the network approves Siar; see [what is open today](/platforms).
- **Publishing and scheduling** with per-network payloads validated against each network's limits, atomic claiming so a post is never sent twice, retries and first comments.
- **Media:** simple, from-URL, large and multipart uploads, with conversion and per-network checks.
- **Accounts and teams:** OAuth for every network behind one endpoint, hosted connect links, channel selection and reconnect detection.
- **Comments, analytics and imports:** comment publishing and import, normalised post and account analytics with raw payloads, post history import and CSV import.
- **Webhooks:** {WEBHOOK_EVENTS.length} signed event types with retries, a delivery log and resend.
- **X credit:** prepaid balance with a hold, capture and release ledger, and a quote endpoint.
- **{operations().length} operations** in one OpenAPI document, served at [/openapi.json](/openapi.json).
- **Tooling:** TypeScript SDK, JSON-first CLI and MCP server (all version 0.1.0), built from the same OpenAPI contract. Not on npm yet.
- **Pricing:** Free (20 posts to try), Pro, Business and Enterprise plans per organization; see [pricing](/pricing).
