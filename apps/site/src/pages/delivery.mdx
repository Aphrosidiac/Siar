{SITE_NAME} is a software service sold by **{LEGAL_NAME}** (company number {LEGAL_REG}, Malaysia). It is an API, a web dashboard and documentation that you use online. We do not sell or ship physical goods.

## Shipping

Nothing is shipped, so there is no shipping address to give, no shipping charge and no delivery time to wait for. The price you see on the [pricing page](/pricing) is the whole price; it has no shipping or handling fee.

## How and when you get access

- **Free plan.** [Create an account]({DASHBOARD_URL}/signup) and you can use the dashboard and the API straight away. No payment is needed.
- **Paid plans.** The plan is switched on automatically once the payment is confirmed. The payment is confirmed by our payment processor: a card payment at once, FPX or GrabPay when your bank reports it. Your organization's limits change as soon as that happens, and you receive a receipt by email. Your invoices are always in Billing in the dashboard.
- **Free trial.** The trial starts as soon as you finish the card step.
- **X credit.** Credit is added to your balance once the top-up payment is confirmed.

Access is delivered on the web, in the [dashboard]({DASHBOARD_URL}), and through the API with your API keys. There is nothing to download or install, except the optional client libraries listed in the [documentation](/docs/sdk-cli-mcp).

## If your plan is not active after paying

Refresh the Billing page first. If the payment went through but the plan has not changed, email [{CONTACT_EMAIL}](mailto:{CONTACT_EMAIL}) or call [{CONTACT_PHONE}](tel:{CONTACT_PHONE_TEL}) with your organization name and the payment reference, and we will fix it. If it cannot be fixed, we refund the payment.

## Returns

Because nothing physical is sold, there is nothing to return. To stop paying, cancel your plan; to ask about a payment, see the [refund and cancellation policy](/refunds).
